Whole project understanding

TeleZyra is a full ecosystem, not just a feed.

💬

Messaging & calls

DMs, groups, channels, file sharing, voice rooms, video calls, message search, reactions, and presence.

🌐

Communities

Spaces, forums, events, resources, moderation, roles, member discovery, and private or public workspaces.

🎬

Short videos & media

Reels, stories, live streams, creator playlists, media galleries, recommendations, and engagement loops.

🛍

Marketplace

Products, services, listings, seller center, orders, reviews, payments, coupons, and commerce analytics.

📈

Creator studio

Calendar, analytics, revenue, brand deals, subscriptions, monetization, campaign management, and payouts.

💼

Professional network

Profiles, education, experience, skills, portfolios, jobs, applications, companies, verification, and resumes.

🤖

AI powered

Smart compose, translation, moderation, summaries, recommendations, assistant workflows, and content insights.

🔐

Trust & safety

Privacy controls, 2FA, reports, appeals, blocked accounts, content review, anti-spam, and security sessions.
