# TeleZyra Project Understanding

TeleZyra is the renamed product direction derived from the Nexora reference boards: an all-in-one connected digital world that combines social networking, real-time messaging, communities, creator tools, commerce, professional networking, AI capabilities, and developer extensibility.

## Product Vision

**Tagline:** Connect · Create · Build · Grow

TeleZyra should feel bigger than a conventional social feed. The product is positioned as a platform where users can maintain one identity while participating in many contexts: personal social activity, creator growth, business commerce, professional networking, communities, developer tools, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Core Areas

1. **Public Landing Experience**
   - Strong futuristic brand identity.
   - Dark blue / neon purple visual direction.
   - Hero messaging around a connected digital world.
   - Metrics, feature pillars, and clear waitlist/get-started CTA.

2. **Main Feed**
   - Left navigation for Home, Explore, Short Videos, Messages, Notifications, Communities, Marketplace, Events, Jobs, Create, and More.
   - Story rail, post composer, multimedia posts, trending panel, people suggestions, and suggested communities.
   - Supports likes, comments, shares, media posts, live posts, and feelings/status updates.

3. **Messaging & Communication**
   - DM, group, community, and chat tabs.
   - Searchable conversations, file sharing, voice notes, and team chats.
   - Voice/video calls and group call interfaces.
   - Presence, notifications, and unread badges.

4. **Profiles & Settings**
   - Deep profile pages with cover images, verified identity, bio, links, stats, media grid, posts, reels, and likes.
   - Settings for account, privacy, security, notifications, language/region, payments, connected apps, data permissions, and advanced options.

5. **Communities / Spaces**
   - Community discovery and workspace screens.
   - Channels, forums, voice rooms, resources, jobs, events, members, and moderation.
   - Roles, member lists, polls, files, and live rooms.

6. **Creator Studio**
   - Dashboard for content analytics, audience insights, revenue, subscriptions, live streaming, store, payouts, and settings.
   - Content calendar and campaign planning.
   - Top-content analysis and engagement tracking.

7. **Marketplace / Seller Center**
   - Product listings, categories, seller center, orders, customers, reviews, analytics, payouts, and inventory/listing management.

8. **Professional Network**
   - Professional profile, experience, education, skills, certifications, projects, services, jobs, applications, companies, events, and newsletters.

9. **Trust & Safety**
   - Report queues, appeals, impersonation checks, spam/fraud detection, content review, account integrity, moderator audit, and blocked accounts.

10. **Developer & Connector Stack**
    - Developer portal, API explorer, OAuth, webhooks, bots, SDKs, usage metrics, permissions, keys, and integrations.
    - Connector categories include auth, payments, media storage/CDN, email, SMS, push notifications, search, analytics, moderation, AI, maps, and webhooks.

## Implementation Layers

- **Client apps:** public web, app shell, mobile UI, admin, creator studio.
- **Shared packages:** design system, UI components, types, API client, utilities.
- **Server services:** auth, users, social graph, posts, messaging, media, communities, marketplace, payments, notifications, AI, search, moderation, analytics.
- **Data layer:** relational data, document/media data, cache, queues, object storage, search indexes, analytics warehouse.
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, CI/CD, CDN, monitoring, logging, metrics, backups, and cloud deployments.

## Design Translation Applied

The website now uses the reference-board visual language but renames the platform to **TeleZyra**:

- Neon blue/purple glassmorphism.
- Blueprint-style page sections.
- Dark-mode product cards.
- Mobile deep-screen mockups for messages, profile, reels, and calls.
- Dashboard mockups for feed, communities, creator studio, and architecture.
- Light-mode SaaS workspace preview.
- Roadmap reflecting V1–V4, V5–V8, and V9–V15 project phases.
